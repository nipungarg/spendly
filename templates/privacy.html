Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide when creating an account (such as your name and email address) and the expense data you enter into Spendly, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Spendly service — displaying your expenses, generating summaries, and enabling you to manage your financial records. We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to any third party.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We take reasonable precautions to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. You may delete your account and all associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third-party services. We may use infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) to operate the app, but these providers do not have access to your expense data beyond what is necessary to run the service.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the app's support channels. We are committed to addressing your concerns promptly.
